## Notes
Each of 24 corpus cases PUT to a local Radicale 3.9.2 (nixpkgs, `.#calendar-spike`) on
loopback with `--auth-type none` and a fresh storage folder, one resource per UID as RFC
4791 section 4.1 requires; read back and compared; then edited as nitya would (every
SUMMARY changed, SEQUENCE bumped; a new EXDATE in case 07, an attendee's answer in 18, the
moved instance moved again in 08), written back with `If-Match` on the ETag, read again
and compared with the edit.

What it showed (`out/caldav-radicale/table.tsv`, `details.md`):
- Meaning and every property kept for all 24 cases, on import and after write-back,
  including THISANDFUTURE ranges, BYSETPOS, RDATE, floating times, X- properties,
  alarms and the timed override of an all-day series.
- No output is byte-identical: Radicale re-serializes (property and parameter order,
  folding points). nitya cannot detect a server-side change by hashing bytes; it must use
  ETags and sync tokens.
- Case 10b sent as one resource with two UIDs is refused with 400 ("Multiple VEVENT
  components with different UIDs"); split per UID it round-trips. A CalDAV driver writes
  one resource per UID.

Not controlled by the script: Radicale is the nixpkgs build in `flake.lock`; loopback
only, no network; port 5299 unless `RADICALE_PORT` is set. A hosted CalDAV provider
(iCloud or similar) is still to be run, and may behave differently.
