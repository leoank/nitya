## Notes

Every corpus case PUT to a throwaway calendar `nitya-spike` in the owner's iCloud
(CalDAV, an app-specific password; the calendar home found from
`current-user-principal` and `calendar-home-set`), one resource per UID with
`If-None-Match: *`; read back with GET and compared; edited as in the Radicale run, PUT
with `If-Match` on the ETag, read again and compared. Events, then the calendar, are
deleted at the end. No conversion: iCloud stores iCalendar.

What it showed:
- Meaning kept for 23 of 24 on import and all 24 on write-back, with every UID kept.
  Unlike Google and Graph, iCloud keeps floating time (04), RDATE (16), RANGE=THISANDFUTURE
  (10a), a cancelled instance (09), URL, ATTACH and X- properties (20) and both alarms
  (21). It reorders RRULE parts (07, 14, 15) and replaces every VTIMEZONE with its own
  (historic RDATEs, X-LIC-LOCATION; same offsets in the window). Like Radicale, it is a
  faithful store of iCalendar.
- Case 18 differs only in the organizer's form: iCloud rewrites ORGANIZER to the
  principal's urn with the address in an EMAIL parameter, and adds SCHEDULE-STATUS to
  the attendee. A driver compares organizers by EMAIL as well as by value.
- Scheduling (prediction 4): iCloud schedules on the server. The owner's Apple ID is
  their Gmail address and the only address iCloud organizes as, so the owner chose their
  Outlook.com account as the attendee. iCloud set SCHEDULE-STATUS=1.2 (delivered), but no
  invitation reached the Outlook.com mailbox (inbox, junk, deleted) nor its calendar in
  the 5 minutes the run waited or 20 minutes after. Why is not known (mail sent by Apple
  on behalf of a gmail.com address may fail Outlook.com's sender checks); with this
  account the reply path could not be tested. A first full run that day, with the Google
  account as attendee, sent nothing, since organizer and attendee were the same address.

Not controlled by the script: iCloud as of the run; mail delivery; the owner's accounts
and secrets (NITYA_SECRETS, default ~/.config/nitya). The first full run also wrote an
account identifier (the principal path) unredacted into out/; it was overwritten, never
committed, and the redaction now covers principal paths and the urn.
